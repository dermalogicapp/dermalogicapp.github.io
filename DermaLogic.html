Before you start

DermaLogic gives general skin-care information and estimates from your camera. It is not a medical device and is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose skin conditions, and results depend on lighting and camera quality.

Your data is saved in this browser. A face photo is sent to YouCam AI only if you turn on cloud analysis; otherwise it is analysed on this device.

If you have a painful, spreading, bleeding or changing skin problem, or signs of an allergic reaction such as swelling of the face, lips or throat, seek medical care.
